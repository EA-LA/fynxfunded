# FYNX trading engine: readiness and how to connect it

The FYNX Trade terminal (`/trade/access`) and the trading engine behind it are built and tested. What's missing is the "fuel": a licensed price feed, a server to host the engine, and the owner's approval to switch trading on. This page lists exactly what to plug in.

## What is built

| Part | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine core | `src/engine/core.ts` | Validates orders (volume steps, price steps, stop/target on the right side, free margin, fresh quotes). Fills market orders at bid/ask and pending limit/stop orders on price. Values profit in dollars for every pair (converts JPY, GBP… through the quote book). Works out equity, margin, free margin and margin level. Closes at stop loss / take profit. Rolls the trading day at 22:00 UTC from the balance at the reset. Enforces the daily loss and static maximum loss limits by closing everything and locking the account. `targetReport` gives profit vs target, trading days, best trading day and the best-day (consistency) share, and a plain list of what is still missing; the profit target is reported once for review only when target, minimum days and the consistency rule (`consistencyPercent`, 40 on FYNX Funded) are all met with nothing open. `modifyOrder` changes the price, stop loss or take profit of a pending order with the same checks as a new order (price on the right side of the market for its type, levels on the right side of the order price). Trading hours per instrument: opening, changing and closing are refused with `market_closed` ("The EURUSD market is closed; it opens Sun 22:00 UTC") while the instrument's market is closed; cancelling a pending order is always allowed. While closed, `tick` fills nothing, hits no stop and checks no loss limit; on the first fresh price after the reopen a stop loss or stop order beyond a weekend gap fills at that price (not at the level), a limit fills at its price or better, and the loss limits are checked on that price. Swap/rollover: at each daily reset, positions held through it accrue the instrument's `swap` rate (USD per lot per night; none on Saturday/Sunday resets, three nights on the triple day, Wednesday by default); the swap stays on the position (in floating profit and equity, so it counts toward the loss limits) and is settled into the balance and the deal's `netProfit` on close, split pro rata on partial closes. `swap_applied` events. Idempotent orders (`clientId`). All money uses `decimal.js`, rounded to cents. Pure functions: no clock, no I/O. |
| Trading hours | `src/engine/hours.ts` | `FOREX_HOURS` (Sunday 17:00 to Friday 17:00 New York, i.e. 22:00 UTC in US winter and 21:00 UTC in summer, US daylight-saving rule built in, no `Intl` needed), `ALWAYS_OPEN`, `isMarketOpen`, `marketState` (open, next open/close time, reason) and host-supplied `closures` (holidays, provider maintenance) with a plain reason. The terminal's open/closed badge, order panel note ("Opens Sun 23:00 your time · in 1d 10h") and the engine use this same schedule. |
| Types | `src/engine/types.ts` | Instruments, quotes, orders, positions, deals, rule sets, account state, events, `EngineError` codes. |
| Venue | `src/engine/venue.ts` | `LocalVenue` runs accounts: connects a `QuoteFeed`, loads/saves through an `AccountStore` with optimistic locking, serialises commands per account, ticks on every command, publishes engine events. `buildSpecs` turns the 45-pair catalogue into instrument specs from venue settings, with `hours` (default `FOREX_HOURS`; `null` only for tests), `hoursBySymbol` overrides and `swap` rates per symbol (validated; pairs left out charge no swap). `symbolMarketState(specs, symbol, now)` and `venue.markets()` (every pair's open/closed state, reason and next change at the venue clock) for `/v1/status`. `rulesFromPurchase` turns an account's stored purchase rules (`profit_target_percent`, `daily_loss_percent`, `max_loss_percent`, `min_trading_days`, `consistency_percent`) into an engine `RuleSet` for `rulesFor`, refusing malformed values. Every snapshot carries a `report` (the target report). `MemoryStore` and `StaticQuoteFeed` exist for tests only. |
| Host skeleton | `src/engine/host.ts` | `handleEngineRequest(request, options)`: the server side of the HTTP contract below, framework-free. Checks the bearer token (`verifyToken`, e.g. Firebase Admin `verifyIdToken`), that the user owns the account (`canAccess`) and, for opening or changing orders, the account's `tradingEnabled` switch (closing and cancelling are always allowed). `GET /v1/status` returns your `status()` plus `markets` from the venue's trading hours. Reads bodies strictly (unknown fields refused), maps `Idempotency-Key` to `clientId`, and returns engine errors as `{ error: { code, message } }` with the right HTTP status. Not imported by the website and not deployed by itself. |
| Firestore store | `src/engine/firestore-store.ts` | `FirestoreAccountStore(getFirestore(), { collection: "engineAccounts" })`: the `AccountStore` for a Firebase host. One document per account; `save` runs in a transaction and refuses with `conflict` unless the stored `sequence` is the one the engine loaded (creation refuses if the document exists). Drops `undefined` fields (Firestore refuses them), refuses states over 900 KB (`state_too_large`, below Firestore's 1 MiB limit), refuses odd account ids and malformed stored documents (`bad_state`). Needs only the Admin SDK's `collection().doc()` and `runTransaction`, so it imports nothing and is not in the site bundle. |
| Price feed template | `src/engine/quote-feed.ts` | `StreamingQuoteFeed({ transport, parse, symbolMap, pipSize })`: the `QuoteFeed` for a licensed provider. The provider adapter only writes `transport.open(symbols, onMessage, onClose)` (WebSocket/SSE/SDK) and `parse(message)` → `{ symbol, bid, ask, time }`. The template maps provider symbols (e.g. `EUR/USD`) to FYNX symbols, refuses bad prices before the engine can fill on them (missing/negative numbers, bid above ask, spread wider than `maxSpreadPips` (50), timestamps in the future or older than the last quote), reconnects with backoff (1 s, 2 s, 5 s, 10 s, 30 s) and reports `health()` (`live` = connected with a valid quote in the last 15 s, accepted/refused counts by reason, reconnects, last error) for `/v1/status` `priceFeed`. |
| Gateways | `src/engine/gateway.ts` | What the terminal talks to: `DisconnectedGateway` (default; nothing is sent), `HttpGateway` (the HTTP contract below) and `LocalGatewayAdapter` (in-process venue). `createGateway` picks one from `VITE_FYNX_TRADE_API_URL`. |
| Terminal wiring | `src/trade-access/useEngine.ts`, `TradeAccess.tsx` | Engine status pill, live bid/ask on SELL/BUY, confirm-to-send order flow (stop/target in pips converted to prices), positions with close buttons and an editor per position (change or remove stop loss / take profit as prices with the dollar result at each level, stop to break-even, warning when a stop is beyond the room left before the loss limit, partial close), pending orders with an editor per order (move the price, set/change/remove stop loss and take profit, distance from the market in pips, dollar result at each level after the fill, warning when a stop is beyond the room left before the loss limit) and cancel, "Progress on this account" card on the Account tab from the snapshot report, per-pair market hours from `/v1/status` `markets` (refreshed every minute; the standard forex week when the server sends none or its answer has passed its next change): a lock and the closure reason (e.g. a holiday) next to closed pairs in Market Watch, the order panel note per pair, and in the position and pending-order editors a plain "market is closed, opens … your time, in …" note with Save, Close part and the row's Close button locked until the open (cancelling a pending order always works), history of deals, account strip from engine metrics (equity, margin, margin level, room to the loss limit). |
| Tests | `src/test/engine-hours-swap.test.ts`, `src/test/engine-market-status.test.ts`, `src/test/trading-engine.test.ts`, `src/test/engine-target-report.test.ts`, `src/test/engine-modify-order.test.ts`, `src/test/engine-host.test.ts`, `src/test/engine-adapters.test.ts`, `src/test/trade-terminal.test.tsx` | Fills, conversions, validation, idempotency, metrics, partial/full close, SL/TP, pending fills, loss-limit lock, daily reset, target report, venue end to end, HTTP contract, target report and 40% best-day rule, purchase rules, terminal place/close, SL/TP edit and partial close through the engine, pending-order changes (engine, venue, gateways, terminal), the host skeleton answering `HttpGateway` end to end (auth, ownership, trading switch, strict bodies, error codes), trading hours (winter/summer and both daylight-saving weeks, next open/close, closures, per-symbol hours), refusals while closed, no fills or stop-outs while closed, weekend-gap fills for stops, stop orders and limits, loss limits on the first price after the reopen, swap nights per reset and triple day, long charged/short paid, pro-rata settlement on partial close, no swap for resets before the position opened, swap rate validation, the terminal's market-closed note on a Saturday, per-symbol market states in `/v1/status` (venue, host, HttpGateway, local adapter, malformed entries dropped, older servers without `markets`), the terminal marking a holiday-closed pair and locking its editors while cancel still works, the Firestore store under a venue (create, trade, reload, lock conflicts, size and shape checks) and the price-feed template (validation, symbol mapping, health, reconnect backoff, clean stop). |

FYNX Funded's official evaluation keeps running on the existing server rule engine (`functions/src/apiRuleEngine.ts`, `fundedPolicy.ts`). The trading engine uses the same rule numbers (daily loss from the 22:00 UTC balance, static maximum loss, profit target, minimum days), so a phase result can be cross-checked against it.

## What to plug in ("the gas")

1. **A licensed price feed** for the 45 forex pairs, with a contract that allows using its prices to fill simulated orders. Use `StreamingQuoteFeed` from `src/engine/quote-feed.ts`: write the provider's `transport.open` and `parse` (about 20 lines for a typical WebSocket API), pass `symbolMap` if the provider names pairs differently and `pipSize: s => FOREX_PAIRS.find(p => p.symbol === s)?.pipSize`, then `venue.connect(feed)` and report `feed.health().live` as `priceFeed` in `status()`. TradingView display widgets cannot be used for this.
2. **A host for the engine.** This is either the FYNX API or a Cloud Function / server that does three things:
   - creates a `LocalVenue` with `buildSpecs(FOREX_PAIRS, { leverage, commissionPerLotPerSide, maxLots, swap })`. Trading hours default to the forex week; pass the provider's published hours as `hours`/`hoursBySymbol` (with holiday `closures`) if they differ. `swap` is the broker's or liquidity provider's overnight rates per symbol in USD per lot per night (negative = charged, `tripleWeekday` if not Wednesday); leave it out and no swap is charged;
   - stores accounts with `new FirestoreAccountStore(getFirestore(), { collection: "engineAccounts" })` from `src/engine/firestore-store.ts` (the `sequence` field is the lock). Add a Firestore rule that denies all client reads/writes on `engineAccounts` (owner-controlled; only the host's Admin SDK touches it);
   - loads each account's purchased rules (`rulesFor`), e.g. `rulesFromPurchase(startingBalance, purchase.rules, { funded })` with `consistency_percent: "40"` for evaluation phases, matching `functions/src/fundedPolicy.ts`.
3. **Run the engine on a timer.** Call `venue.snapshot(accountId)` for open accounts on every quote batch, or at least every second. That timer step is what fills pending orders and hits stops when nobody is clicking.
4. **Venue settings to confirm:** leverage, commission per lot per side, maximum lots, maximum quote age, and trading hours per symbol.
5. **Expose the HTTP contract below**, with Firebase ID-token authentication. Each user may only reach their own accounts. The quickest way: in the server, call `handleEngineRequest({ method, path, headers, body }, { venue, verifyToken, canAccess, tradingEnabled, status })` from `src/engine/host.ts` and send back its `status` and JSON `body`. HTTP statuses: 401 sign-in, 403 not your account / trading switched off / account closed, 404 unknown account, position or order, 405 wrong method, 409 conflict, 400 malformed body, 422 any other engine refusal, 500 unexpected.
6. **Set `VITE_FYNX_TRADE_API_URL`** in Vercel (an `https://` address) and add that host to `connect-src` in the site's Content-Security-Policy (`vercel.json`). These are owner-controlled settings.
7. **Switch trading on per account:** `fundedTradeAccess` must return `tradingEnabled: true` for accounts that may trade. Until then the terminal never sends an order, even when connected.

## HTTP contract (`HttpGateway`)

All requests use `Authorization: Bearer <Firebase ID token>`. Every response is JSON. A successful action returns a **Snapshot**: `{ state: AccountState, metrics: AccountMetrics, quotes: { [symbol]: Quote }, report?: TargetReport }`. `report` is optional; when it is present the terminal shows the progress card (LocalVenue always includes it).

| Method | Path | Body | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GET | `/v1/status` | | `{ priceFeed: boolean, execution: boolean, message, markets? }` — `markets` is `{ [symbol]: { open, nextChange (ms or null), reason (null when open) } }`; the host skeleton fills it from `venue.markets()`, so holiday and maintenance closures entered as `closures` in the hours reach the terminal. Optional: without it the terminal uses the standard forex week. |
| GET | `/v1/accounts/{id}` | | Runs the timer step, returns the snapshot |
| POST | `/v1/accounts/{id}/orders` | `OrderRequest` | `Idempotency-Key` header = `clientId` |
| POST | `/v1/accounts/{id}/positions/{positionId}/close` | `{ lots? }` | Partial close when `lots` is given |
| PATCH | `/v1/accounts/{id}/positions/{positionId}` | `{ stopLoss?, takeProfit? }` | `null` removes a level |
| PATCH | `/v1/accounts/{id}/orders/{orderId}` | `{ price?, stopLoss?, takeProfit? }` | Changes a pending order; `null` removes a level. Volume, side and type cannot change |
| DELETE | `/v1/accounts/{id}/orders/{orderId}` | | Cancels a pending order |

Errors: HTTP 4xx/5xx with `{ "error": { "code": "no_margin", "message": "Not enough free margin…" } }`. The terminal shows the message as it is. Codes come from `EngineError` (`market_closed`, `bad_settings`, `bad_rules`, `unknown_symbol`, `no_quote`, `stale_quote`, `bad_volume`, `bad_price`, `bad_stop`, `bad_target`, `no_margin`, `account_closed`, `no_position`, `no_order`, `conflict`), plus from the host skeleton `unauthorized`, `forbidden`, `trading_disabled`, `bad_request`, `not_found`, `method_not_allowed`, `server_error`, and from the Firestore store `bad_state`, `state_too_large`.

## Still owner decisions

- Price-feed provider and contract.
- Spreads/markups, leverage, commission, and the swap rates per pair (the engine applies them at the 22:00 UTC reset once given; FYNX may also decide to charge no swap on evaluation accounts by leaving them out).
- Holiday closures (e.g. 25 December, 1 January) as published by the price provider: pass them as `closures` in the trading hours.
- Whether a passed phase stops trading automatically or waits for review (today: reported, waits for review).
- Account number + password sign-in by email (requested 2026-11-07): needs credential issuing in the backend and an approved release. Today customers sign in with their FYNX login.
